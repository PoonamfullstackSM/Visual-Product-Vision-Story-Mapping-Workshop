
# Packers & Movers Product Vision & Story Mapping Workshop 🚚

A visual product discovery and planning workshop for a **Packers & Movers relocation platform**. This project demonstrates how product vision, customer needs, business goals, roadmaps, stakeholder feedback, and user stories can be aligned before development begins.

Built using **Roman Pichler Product Vision Board, Goal-Oriented Roadmapping, Miro, Jira, and RACI-based stakeholder planning**.

## 🎯 Project Overview

The objective of this workshop is to create a clear product direction and transform customer needs into a prioritized product backlog.

### Product Vision

> To erase the stress of shifting worldwide, ensuring every family and enterprise steps into their next chapter with complete peace of mind.

The platform is designed to support customers through a smoother relocation journey by addressing common moving challenges such as planning, trust, coordination, transparency, and service selection.

## 🗂️ Workshop Artifacts

This repository includes the following product discovery and planning artifacts:

1. **Product Vision Board**  
   Identifies target groups, customer needs, product features, and business goals using the Roman Pichler template.

2. **Product Roadmap – Strategic View**  
   A goal-oriented roadmap covering MVP, Release 1, and Release 2.

3. **Product Roadmap – Execution View**  
   A Jira-based roadmap showing epics and planned delivery timelines.

4. **Persona Empathy Maps**  
   Empathy maps for Families and Students, covering their:
   - Says
   - Thinks
   - Does
   - Feels
   - Goals
   - Pain points

5. **User Story Mapping**  
   A Miro-based story map showing MVP scope and future backlog items.

6. **Stakeholder Review Framework**  
   An asynchronous review and feedback process using stakeholder input and dot-voting.

7. **Decision Log & RACI Matrix**  
   Captures stakeholder feedback, decisions, ownership, and accountability.

## 🔄 Product Discovery Approach

The workshop follows a structured product discovery flow:

1. Define the product vision
2. Identify target customers and user needs
3. Create customer personas and empathy maps
4. Identify product capabilities and features
5. Develop a strategic product roadmap
6. Convert features into user stories
7. Map stories into MVP and future releases
8. Collect stakeholder feedback
9. Prioritize ideas through dot-voting
10. Document decisions and ownership using a RACI matrix
